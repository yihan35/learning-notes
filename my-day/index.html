这期的重点不是追求“说得高级”，而是练习把自己经历过的事情用英语顺着说出来。描述一天不需要先想观点或高级内容，只要回忆：几点起床、做了什么、上午和下午在忙什么、晚上如何收尾。

01为什么从 My Day 开始

“描述自己的一天”是最不需要构思内容的口语练习。练习材料本来就来自自己的生活，因此可以把注意力放在英语表达和句子连接上。

先记住：我今天几点起床？起床后做了什么？上午、下午、晚上分别在做什么？这些都是真正经历过的事情，也是之后各种口语输出的基础。

02今日语料｜描述一天

不用背整篇范文，先准备一批能反复组合的表达。

语料中文意思例句
wake up醒来；起床I usually wake up around 8 on weekdays.我平时工作日大概 8 点醒。
stretch out伸展身体；伸个懒腰I usually stretch out a little before I get out of bed.我通常会在下床前伸展一下身体。
get ready for the day为一天做好准备I take a shower and get ready for the day.我洗个澡，然后开始为一天做准备。
grab a quick breakfast简单快速地吃个早餐I usually grab a quick breakfast before I start working.我通常开始工作前简单吃点早餐。
get into work mode进入工作状态It takes me a while to get into work mode in the morning.我早上需要一会儿才能进入工作状态。
get started on...开始做……I usually get started on my work around 9.我通常 9 点左右开始工作。
take a break休息一下I usually take a break around lunchtime.我通常午饭时间会休息一下。
run a few errands办几件杂事；跑几个地方办事I went out to run a few errands in the afternoon.我下午出去办了几件杂事。
get some fresh air出去透透气I like to go outside and get some fresh air after working for a few hours.工作几个小时后，我喜欢出去透透气。
slow down放慢节奏I usually slow down a little in the evening.我晚上通常会让自己的节奏慢下来一点。
wind down放松下来；逐渐进入休息状态I usually read for a while to wind down before bed.睡前我通常会看一会儿书，让自己慢慢放松下来。
call it a day收工；结束今天的工作I usually call it a day around six.我通常六点左右就收工了。

描述一天的主线

wake up→get ready→breakfast→work→break→errands→fresh air→slow down→wind down→call it a day

03今日框架｜把一天串起来

I've been + doing + lately

我最近一直在……

描述最近一段时间的生活状态或变化。

I've been waking up earlier lately.

我最近一直起得比较早。

I've been trying to slow down a little lately.

我最近一直在尝试让生活节奏慢一点。

I usually + 动词...

我通常会……

描述习惯或日常安排。

I usually get started on my work around nine.

我通常九点左右开始工作。

I usually wind down around ten.

我通常十点左右开始放松下来。

After + doing..., I...

做完……之后，我……

连接一天中的不同活动。

After getting some fresh air, I usually get back to work.

出去透透气之后，我通常会回去继续工作。

By the time..., I...

等到……的时候，我已经……

补充某个时间点的状态，让表达更有层次。

By the time I finish work, I'm usually pretty tired.

等我结束工作的时候，通常已经挺累了。

04四轮练习｜从模仿到迁移

Round 1 · 先把一天说出来

场景：描述一个普通工作日，可以按早上、上午、中午、下午、晚上来讲。

目标：尽量用上今日语料，先把一天说出来，不追求复杂。

I usually wake up around eight and stretch out a little before I get out of bed. Then I take a shower, get ready for the day, and grab a quick breakfast. It usually takes me a while to get into work mode, but once I do, I get started on my work. Around lunchtime, I take a break and sometimes go outside to get some fresh air. In the evening, I try to slow down and wind down a little. I usually call it a day around six or seven.

我通常八点左右醒来，下床前伸展一下。然后洗澡、为一天做准备，再简单吃点早餐。我早上需要一点时间进入工作状态，进入状态后就开始工作。午饭时间会休息，有时出去透透气。晚上尽量放慢节奏、放松下来，通常六七点左右收工。

Round 2 · 同一天，说得更丰富

场景：仍然讲普通的一天，不换语料。

目标：给句子加上时间、原因或补充信息，回答“什么时候？为什么？之后怎么样？”

because...so...but...after...before...which means...especially when...

I usually wake up around eight, but I've been waking up a little earlier lately because I want to have more time in the morning. I stretch out a little, take a shower, and get ready for the day before I grab a quick breakfast. It usually takes me a while to get into work mode, especially when I didn't sleep well, but once I do, I get started on my work. I usually take a break around lunchtime, and if the weather is nice, I go outside to get some fresh air. By the time I finish work, I'm usually ready to slow down and just have a quiet evening. I might watch something or read for a while to wind down before bed.

Round 3 · 换个场景，重新调用语料

场景：一个比较忙的日子：早上起晚了、没时间吃早餐、事情很多、下午出去办事、晚上终于能休息。

目标：不学新表达，把同一批语料放进不同情境里。

尽量使用：wake up / get ready for the day / get started on / take a break / run a few errands / get some fresh air / slow down / wind down / call it a day

I woke up pretty late today, so I didn't really have time to grab a quick breakfast. I just got ready for the day and got started on my work. It was a pretty busy morning, so I barely had time to take a break. In the afternoon, I went out to run a few errands and get some fresh air, which actually helped me feel better. By the evening, I was exhausted, so I just wanted to slow down, wind down, and call it a day early.

Round 4 · 讲你自己真实的一天

主题：My Day。根据自己的真实生活作答，不再依赖完整情境。

  • 几点醒？早上第一件事是什么？
  • 什么时候开始工作或学习？
  • 中间会不会休息，有没有出门？
  • 下午通常是什么状态？
  • 什么时候结束工作，晚上怎么放松？
  • 最近生活有没有发生变化？

练习要求：尽量使用 6 个以上今日语料，至少使用 2 个今日框架；尝试用 I've been + doing + lately 讲最近的变化，并用 By the time... 描述一个时间点的状态。

I've been waking up a little earlier lately, so I usually wake up around eight and stretch out for a few minutes before I get out of bed. Then I take a shower, get ready for the day, and have some breakfast before I get started on my work. I usually work for a few hours and then take a break, especially if I've been sitting at my desk for too long. Sometimes I'll go outside to get some fresh air or run a few errands.

By the time I finish work, I'm usually pretty tired, so I try to slow down in the evening. I might make dinner, watch something, or just listen to music while I wind down. I don't always have a super productive day, but I like having this kind of rhythm because it gives me some structure without making my whole day feel too busy.

05最后复盘｜把熟悉变成会用

一批语料 → Round 1 简单使用 → Round 2 增加细节 → Round 3 换场景重新调用 → Round 4 回到自己的真实生活

同一批表达重复使用 3–4 次后，才更容易从“我认识这个表达”变成“我说话时能想得起来”。My Day 只是第一次练习的载体；之后换成旅行、工作、健身、社交或生活选择，也可以沿用这个训练方式。
